# Lokaria

Lokaria is a booking platform where players find and book sports venues and creative spaces across Indonesia, and venue owners manage their schedules and payments.

## Language

### Places

**Category**:
A kind of bookable activity, such as Football & Futsal, Padel, or Music Studio. Every Space belongs to exactly one Category.
_Avoid_: Sport, sport world, discipline

**Venue**:
A partner business at one address that offers one or more Spaces to book.
_Avoid_: Club, facility, location

**Space**:
A single bookable unit inside a Venue, such as a court, pitch, studio room or pond.
_Avoid_: Court (as the generic term), room, unit

**City**:
The area a Venue is listed under for browsing and filtering.
_Avoid_: Floor, area, region

### Money & loyalty

**Credit**:
Prepaid booking balance, where 1 Credit = Rp 10.000, bundled with a Membership Tier and spendable at any Venue.
_Avoid_: Token, voucher, balance (unqualified)

**Point**:
A Lokaria Rewards loyalty unit earned on every Booking, including Bookings paid with Credits, and redeemed for free hours, vouchers or upgrades. Points are never bought.
_Avoid_: Credit, coin, reward (as a unit)

**Membership Tier**:
A 30-day paid plan (Starter, Pro, Elite, marketed as "Lokaria Plus") that grants Credits, a member discount and a longer booking window.
_Avoid_: Subscription, package, plan
